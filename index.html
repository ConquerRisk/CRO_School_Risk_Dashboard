CRO reading: the limit shrinks positions after VaR rises, so it trims a drawn-out crisis but not a sudden shock. Exceptions fall on the same days with or without the limit: it changes the size of the bet, not the accuracy of the model.

Cyber risk

How likely is a large breach after our controls? 300 reported breaches of 30,000+ records, 2004–22, all sectors.

Assumptions and board appetite

Yearly chance of a breach larger than N records

Before controls (inherent) and after (residual); diamonds: board appetite

Reported large breaches by cause

Hacked and unknown causes count as external attacks; the rest as preventable by first-line controls

CRO reading: controls cut the chance by about 40%, far short of the appetite. Meeting it would take almost perfect defence against outside attacks. Transfer the residual (insurance), have the board accept it, or revisit the appetite. The breach frequency is an assumption the CRO must own.
